About

Kegeyli District Specialized School is a state school specializing in exact and natural sciences. It is part of the Agency for Specialized Educational Institutions and prepares students for higher education, subject olympiads and international certificates.

The school has its own canteen for students. It is a day school, not a boarding school, so there is no dormitory.

Frequently asked questions

Does the school have a dormitory?

No. It is a day school, not a boarding school, so there is no dormitory. The school has its own canteen for students.

How are students admitted?

By entrance test: to grade 5 in exact sciences and to grade 7 in natural sciences. Applications are usually submitted online in May–June via my.gov.uz and ariza.piima.uz.

Where is the school located?

9 Kegeyli Gúzari, Kegeyli town, Republic of Karakalpakstan, Uzbekistan.

How can I prepare to study abroad?

The school counsellor helps students choose universities and prepare for IELTS/SAT, essays and scholarships. Open the Study abroad section of this site: it has a roadmap, deadline calendar, document checklist and cost calculator.

What are the school’s working hours?

Monday to Saturday, 08:30–17:00.
