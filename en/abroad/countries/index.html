🇺🇸United StatesStrongest universities; need-based aid at the top$$$
CostPrivate universities are the most expensive, but top universities with generous need-based aid can cost much less (see Top 10)
LanguageEnglish
TestsSAT or ACT at most top universities; English test depends on the university
Work while studyingOn campus up to 20 hours a week in term, full-time in vacations
After graduationOPT: 12 months of work, plus 24 more months for STEM degrees
ScholarshipsNeed-based aid at top universities; Harvard, MIT, Stanford and Caltech say they meet full need
How to applyCommon App; early deadlines around 1 November, regular deadlines in early January
Official site →
🇬🇧United Kingdom3-year degrees through UCAS; Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, UCL$$$
CostInternational tuition is high and depends on the course; check each university
Money for the visaLiving costs to show: £1,171 a month outside London and £1,529 in London (up to 9 months); £1,203 and £1,570 for applications from 30 Nov 2026
LanguageEnglish
TestsIELTS Academic (normally 7.5 for Cambridge); A levels or an equivalent certificate; extra tests for Oxford and Cambridge
Work while studying20 hours a week in term, full-time in vacations
After graduationGraduate route: 2 years, but 18 months for applications from 1 Jan 2027
ScholarshipsFew scholarships for undergraduates; check each university
How to applyUCAS: 15 Oct 2026 for Oxford, Cambridge and medicine; 13 Jan 2027 for the rest
Official site →
🇨🇦CanadaWork options and a post-study work permit; but a national cap$$$
CostTuition varies by province and programme; check each university
Money for the visaAt least CA$22,895 for living plus first-year tuition (since 1 Sept 2025)
LanguageEnglish (French in Quebec)
TestsEnglish test depends on the university
Work while studyingUp to 24 hours a week off campus in term
After graduationPost-graduation work permit (PGWP): 1–3 years depending on the programme
How to applyA national cap of about 155,000 new study permits in 2026; most public institutions need a provincial attestation letter (PAL/TAL)
Official site →
🇩🇪GermanyNo tuition at public universities$$$
CostPublic universities charge no tuition, only a semester contribution; private universities about €5,000–20,000 a year
Money for the visa€11,904 in a blocked account (€992 a month) in 2026
LanguageMost bachelor programmes are in German (usually level C1); some English-taught programmes exist
TestsA German C1 certificate for German-taught programmes; check on the DAAD site whether your certificate is recognised
Work while studying140 full days or 280 half days a year
After graduationA residence permit of up to 18 months to find a job
ScholarshipsDAAD and university scholarships; check the DAAD site
Official site →
🇰🇷South KoreaGKS-U: a full government scholarship$$$
CostUniversities charge tuition; the GKS-U scholarship covers it
LanguageKorean- or English-taught programmes; GKS-U includes a one-year Korean language course
TestsTOPIK; with TOPIK level 5 or 6 you can skip the language year
ScholarshipsGKS-U: tuition, a monthly stipend, airfare and the Korean language course; 1 year language + 4–6 years degree
How to applyThrough the embassy track; check the Korean embassy and studyinkorea.go.kr
Official site →
🇯🇵JapanMEXT: the Japanese government scholarship$$$
LanguageOften Japanese; MEXT students usually study Japanese first
TestsMEXT: a written exam and an interview; a language certificate (IELTS, TOEFL or JLPT) if you have one
ScholarshipsMEXT: living expenses, university fees and airfare
How to applyThrough the Embassy of Japan; at other embassies the deadline for the 2027 programme was in mid-May 2026 — ask the embassy in Uzbekistan for exact dates
Official site →
🇹🇷TürkiyeTürkiye Burslari: full scholarship with housing$$$
CostFree tuition with the scholarship
LanguageTurkish (a one-year course); some programmes in English
ScholarshipsTürkiye Burslari: tuition, accommodation, a monthly stipend, health insurance, a flight and a one-year Turkish course
How to applyIn 2026: 10 January to 20 February; once a year, online at turkiyeburslari.gov.tr, no fee
Official site →
🇭🇺HungaryStipendium Hungaricum: grants for Uzbek students$$$
CostTuition-free with the scholarship
LanguageAbout 900 foreign-language programmes at 30 institutions in 2026/27 (many in English)
ScholarshipsNo tuition, a monthly stipend of HUF 43,700 (about €110) for bachelor’s level, a dormitory or housing allowance, and health insurance
How to applyIt usually opens in November and closes on 15 January (14:00 CET in 2026); apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu. 110 Uzbek students were awarded in 2026/27, with the quota planned to rise to 200
Official site →
🇲🇾MalaysiaEnglish-taught and more affordable; branch campuses$$$
CostPublic universities about RM 10,000–30,000 a year, branch campuses RM 30,000–90,000; living in Kuala Lumpur RM 1,500–2,500 a month
Money for the visaFor the Student Pass, budget roughly RM 2,000 a month in proof of funds, and about RM 1,500–3,000 in EMGS fees
LanguageEnglish
Work while studyingLimited part-time work: up to 20 hours a week in semester breaks
After graduationGraduate Pass: up to 12 months for eligible graduates
ScholarshipsThe Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) is mainly for postgraduates
How to applyThe university applies for your Student Pass through EMGS, and you receive a VAL before you travel
Official site →
🇸🇬SingaporeNUS: a top-10 university; merit scholarships$$$
LanguageEnglish
TestsSAT, AP or ACT, and IELTS or TOEFL scores are submitted if available; requirements depend on the programme
ScholarshipsNUS International Undergraduate Scholarship: citizens of all countries except Singapore, considered through the admission application
How to applyNUS: for international qualifications the application window runs December to February (last cycle 3 Dec 2025 – 23 Feb 2026); application fee S$20
Official site →

Every country needs a student visa to study; visa-free entry for short visits does not let you study.
