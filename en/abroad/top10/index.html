Top 10 universities in the world

RoadmapMy planMy applicationsScholarshipsCountriesCalendarChecklistCalculatorSATEssayTop 10Common AppUCASIELTS

These are the top 10 universities in the QS World University Rankings 2027 (published 18 June 2026). The details come from each university’s official admissions website (checked November 2026). Other rankings, such as THE 2027 where Oxford is first, order the universities differently.

Deadlines, amounts and rules change every year. Check the university’s official site before you apply.

1MIT — Massachusetts Institute of TechnologyCambridge, Massachusetts, USA · Founded 1861
QS 2027: #1

How to apply

PlatformMIT application portal
Application fee$75
Deadlines
Early round 01.11Regular round 04.01

Tests and requirements

  • SAT or ACT required
  • 2 teacher letters required; counselor letter optional
  • An Educational Counselor may offer an interview; if none is available it does not affect your application

English

  • Strongly recommended for some non-native speakers, not required for everyone

Money and aid

  • Aid is need-based; MIT says it meets 100% of demonstrated need. The aid application is separate: 30 Nov (early) or 15 Feb (regular)

Good to know

  • One application per entry year; if you are denied in Early Action, you cannot apply in Regular Action
Official admissions site →
2Imperial College LondonLondon, United Kingdom · Founded 1907
QS 2027: #2

How to apply

PlatformUCAS

Tests and requirements

  • Entry requirements are course-specific; check your country on the official site

English

  • The level depends on the course; IELTS results are valid for 2 years

Money and aid

  • Imperial Inspires scholarships exist for 2027 entry; see the official funding page for more
Official admissions site →
2Stanford UniversityStanford, California, USA · Founded 1885
QS 2027: #2

How to apply

PlatformCommon App
Application fee$100
Deadlines
Early round 15.10 / 01.11Regular round 05.12 / 05.01

Tests and requirements

  • SAT or ACT required
  • 2 teacher letters, a counselor letter and the School Report

English

  • Not required; you may self-report scores, but fluency in English is a prerequisite

Money and aid

  • Not need-blind for international students: asking for aid is a factor in admission. It meets the full demonstrated need of everyone admitted; no merit scholarships (limited athletic ones only)

Good to know

  • Midyear transcript is due 15 Feb; the reply deadline is 1 May
Official admissions site →
4University of OxfordOxford, United Kingdom · Teaching since about 1096
QS 2027: #4

How to apply

PlatformUCAS
Deadlines
UCAS 15.10

Tests and requirements

  • Admissions tests and interviews depend on the course; shortlisted applicants are interviewed

English

  • IELTS Academic is recommended; IELTS Online and IELTS General are not accepted

Money and aid

  • International students must show how they will fund tuition and living costs before a place is confirmed; fees depend on the course

Good to know

  • About 20,000 applications for roughly 3,250 places a year; 20% of undergraduates are international
Official admissions site →
5Harvard UniversityCambridge, Massachusetts, USA · Founded 1636
QS 2027: #5

How to apply

PlatformCommon App
Application fee$95
Deadlines
Early round 01.11Regular round 01.01

Tests and requirements

  • SAT or ACT required (in exceptional cases AP, IB or national exam results can substitute)

English

  • Not required, but you may submit scores

Money and aid

  • Aid is the same for all nationalities and based on need; Harvard meets demonstrated need. Admission decisions are made without regard to whether you apply for aid, including for foreign citizens

Good to know

  • You may self-report test scores; students who enroll must submit official scores
Official admissions site →
6University of CambridgeCambridge, United Kingdom · Founded 1209
QS 2027: #6

How to apply

PlatformUCAS + My Cambridge Application
Deadlines
UCAS 15.10.2026My Cambridge Application 22.10.2026Transcript (international applicants) 22.10.2026

Tests and requirements

  • Some courses require an admission test (UCAT, LNAT, ESAT, TMUA); you may need to register before the UCAS deadline

English

  • IELTS Academic: normally 7.5 overall, usually 7.0 or above in each part

Money and aid

  • Fees depend on the course and are fixed for its duration; no priority is given to students who pay the overseas rate

Good to know

  • The deadline is the same for international and UK applicants; there is no earlier international interview scheme
Official admissions site →
7California Institute of Technology (Caltech)Pasadena, California, USA · Founded 1891
QS 2027: #7

How to apply

PlatformCommon App / QuestBridge
Deadlines
Early round 01.11.2026Regular round 04.01.2027Reply deadline 01.05.2027

Tests and requirements

  • SAT or ACT required; AP or IB scores if taken

English

  • Required (unless you studied in English): IELTS 7 total and 7 in each part, or Duolingo 130 total and in each area

Money and aid

  • Need-aware for international applicants: asking for aid may affect the decision. It meets the full demonstrated need of admitted students. You must request aid when you apply

Good to know

  • Decisions: mid-December (early) and mid-March (regular); reply deadline 1 May 2027
Official admissions site →
8ETH ZurichZurich, Switzerland · Founded 1855
QS 2027: #8

How to apply

PlatformETH online application
Deadlines
Application deadline 31.03

Tests and requirements

  • Many foreign certificates require passing the ETH entrance examination (depends on the certificate)

English

  • German language requirements must be met by the application deadline

Money and aid

  • From autumn 2025, students with a foreign qualification pay CHF 2,190 per semester (it was CHF 730)

Good to know

  • Bachelor’s studies can only start in the autumn semester
Official admissions site →
8UCL (University College London)London, United Kingdom · Founded 1826
QS 2027: #8

How to apply

PlatformUCAS

Tests and requirements

  • Offers are based on three A levels (standard offers A*A*A to ABB depending on the course); other countries’ qualifications are checked for equivalence

English

  • There are 5 levels — check the level for your course; IELTS One Skill Retake is not accepted

Money and aid

  • International fees and scholarships are on the official site and vary by course

Good to know

  • For 2025 entry UCL received nearly 91,000 applications via UCAS
Official admissions site →
10National University of Singapore (NUS)Singapore, Singapore · Founded 1905
QS 2027: #10

How to apply

PlatformNUS Applicant Portal
Application feeS$20
Deadlines
Application period (last year) 03.12.2025 – 23.02.2026

Tests and requirements

  • SAT, AP or ACT scores are submitted if available; requirements vary by programme

English

  • Submit IELTS or TOEFL scores if available; some programmes ask for a high level

Money and aid

  • NUS International Undergraduate Scholarship: candidates are considered through the admission application

Good to know

  • Admission is holistic: academic merit, interests and aptitudes are considered
