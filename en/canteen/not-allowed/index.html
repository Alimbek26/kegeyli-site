Foods not allowed at school

The official list of foods and dishes not allowed in general education schools. Please don’t bring these to school.

38foods and dishes
7groups
Official document (PDF)The full list ↓

Leave these at home

What children most often bring from home — none of it is allowed.

  • Chips
  • Fizzy drinks
  • Energy drinks
  • Chewing gum
  • Hot dogs, burgers
  • Home-made food

The full list

Drinks

5
  • Coloured and clear fizzy drinks
  • Energy and stimulant drinks, natural coffee, alcohol
  • Kvass
  • Fruit and berry cold drinks and mors without heat treatment
  • Kumis and fermented milk drinks with over 0.5% alcohol

Snacks and sweets

6
  • Chips (crisps)
  • Snacks with artificial preservatives (kiriyeshki, crackers)
  • Sweets with artificial flavours, colours and aromas
  • Cream cakes and pastries
  • Chewing gum
  • Kernels of stone fruits

Fast food and sauces

3
  • Burgers, cheeseburgers and hot dogs with meat, mayonnaise, ketchup
  • Spicy sauces, ketchup, mayonnaise, canned and pickled vegetables and fruit
  • Vinegar, mustard, horseradish, hot pepper and other spicy seasonings

Meat and sausages

8
  • Meat, fish and poultry without veterinary inspection
  • All offal except liver, tongue and heart
  • Poultry not cleaned of entrails
  • Aspic, head-meat rolls, liver and blood sausages
  • Raw-smoked meats and sausages, including boiled sausage
  • Meat, poultry and fish dishes without heat treatment
  • Dishes topped with meat or fish, herring-and-potato forshmak
  • Pâté, meat and cottage-cheese pancakes

Dairy and eggs

5
  • Unpasteurised milk and dairy products
  • Unpasteurised cottage cheese and sour cream (from churns)
  • Home-made yoghurt (qatiq)
  • Dirty or cracked eggs, eggs from salmonella-risk farms
  • Fried eggs

Dishes and ingredients

5
  • Okroshka and cold soups
  • Navy-style pasta with mince or chopped egg
  • Instant meals from dry food concentrates
  • Mushrooms and mushroom products
  • Cooking fats, lard, margarine and hydrogenated fats

Spoiled and home-made food

6
  • All home-made (non-factory) food
  • Expired and poor-quality food
  • Leftovers cooked the day before
  • Stale or spoiled vegetables
  • Damaged, bulging, rusty or unlabelled canned goods
  • Grains, flour and dried fruit infested by pests

Nothing found — this product isn’t on the list.

Why it matters

Health

Artificial additives, excess sugar and fat harm a growing body.

Food safety

Unchecked or spoiled food can cause food poisoning.

Focus in class

Caffeine and energy drinks disturb sleep and concentration.

Better choices

The school canteen serves a hot meal every school day.

  • Clean water
  • Fresh fruit
  • Tea
  • A hot meal in the canteen

For parents: give your child water and fruit — the canteen takes care of the rest.
