From Grade 9 to university

What the student does at each stage, and how the counselor helps.

  1. 1

    Grade 9

    Discover

    Student

    • Explore your interests and strongest subjects
    • Build your English towards IELTS
    • Try olympiads

    Counselor

    • First one-to-one conversation
    • Helps choose a direction
    • Introduces options in Uzbekistan and abroad
  2. 2

    Grade 10

    Prepare

    Student

    • Take IELTS: free registration at school
    • Prepare for the SAT if aiming at the USA
    • National certificates, activities and summer projects

    Counselor

    • Builds a long list of universities
    • Plans the testing calendar
    • Advises on activities and scholarships
  3. 3

    Grade 11 · Sep–Jan

    Apply

    Student

    • Finalise your list
    • Write and revise your essays
    • Apply via Common App, UCAS or university portals

    Counselor

    • Gives feedback on essays (the student writes them)
    • Writes the recommendation letter
    • Sends transcript and School Profile, tracks deadlines
  4. 4

    Grade 11 · Feb–Aug

    Decide

    Student

    • Compare offers and grants
    • Sit the national admission tests
    • Visa, if studying abroad

    Counselor

    • Helps compare offers and costs
    • Supports national admission
    • Prepares school documents for the visa
  5. 5

    After graduation

    Stay in touch

    Student

    • Share your result and story with younger students

    Counselor

    • Keeps alumni records
    • Invites alumni to meet current students

What we offer

For students and families

  • One-to-one meetings for students and parents
  • A university list in Uzbekistan and abroad
  • Feedback on essays and applications, never written for you
  • Free IELTS registration at school (official Registration Point)
  • Scholarship and grant search
  • Deadline tracking and reminders

For universities

  • School Profile (PDF): curriculum, grading, results
  • Official transcript in English, with the 5-point scale explained
  • Counselor and teacher recommendations, sent directly
  • Verification of grades and documents on request
  • College Board (CEEB) code: requested; until issued, please list the school by name

Documents and verification: ixm013@piima.uz

Our principles

Honesty

Grades and achievements are reported exactly as they are. Every result on this website can be checked.

The student’s choice

We advise; the student and family decide.

Students’ own work

We give feedback, but essays are always written by the student.

Free for every student

Counselling and IELTS registration cost our students nothing.

Privacy

Students’ documents and personal details are never published.

Equal attention

Every student gets support, not only the top ones.
