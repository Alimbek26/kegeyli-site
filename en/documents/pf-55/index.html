Decree PF-55

Decree of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan

«On measures to expand the network of specialized schools and further improve their activities»

Number
PF-55
Adopted
1 April 2024
Registered
QMMB: 06/24/55/0263, 03.04.2024
Download PDFOn lex.uz →

Purpose

The decree aims to raise free-thinking young people in the schools of the Agency for Specialized Educational Institutions who can compete with their peers worldwide in exact and natural sciences, IT and robotics, and to develop students’ talents.

What it says, in plain words

  1. 1Academicians teach lessons

    From 2024/2025, academicians of the Academy of Sciences teach creativity lessons in Agency schools, help students with practical projects, introduce them to research laboratories and set up “Young Academicians” clubs.

  2. 2STEM councils and a Graduates’ Council

    STEM subject councils of teachers and a Graduates’ Council are set up under the Agency. New STEM programmes are introduced only in agreement with the subject councils, and a Graduates’ Congress is held once a year.

  3. 3“Step into Invention” contest

    Every year a contest is held among school students to create a new product or service in education, IT, green energy, ecology, climate change or agriculture. Winning projects receive funding to be put into practice.

  4. 4Subjects taught in English: +100% bonus

    From 2024/2025, teachers in creative and specialized schools who hold a B2 or higher certificate may teach subjects in a foreign language. Those teaching mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, science or IT in a foreign language receive a monthly bonus of 100% of their base rate.

  5. 5Presidential schools: +350% bonus

    Local teachers at Presidential schools receive a monthly bonus of 350% of their base rate, if they complete 3 Cambridge courses (Introductory, Extension, Marking), have at least one year of work there and hold a C1-level language certificate.

  6. 6Patriotic education

    Outside lessons: grades 5–8 learn about great commanders and the skills of defending the homeland; grades 9–11 take part in military games, sports competitions and meetings with servicemen. There is also an annual “Turon Tigers” military-sports contest, national heritage corners and a “Turon” theatre studio.

  7. 7Engineering programme

    From 2025/2026, the Mirzo Ulugbek school runs a 5-year STEM engineering programme with New Uzbekistan University. Students are selected from 6th-grade graduates, and those who complete it successfully can continue from the 2nd year of the university’s bachelor programme.

  8. 88 new schools and branches

    In 2025–2026, 8 new specialized schools are built in the Agency system (Romitan, Mirzachul, Mirishkor, Davlatabad, Khavast, Tashkent district, Kibray and Gurlan). Branches of the Muhammad al-Khwarizmi school open on the basis of 13 boarding schools.

What it means for us

  • For students: lessons with academicians, “Young Academicians” clubs, the “Step into Invention” contest and military-sports competitions.
  • For teachers: the chance to teach subjects in English with a B2+ certificate and earn a 100% bonus.
  • For graduates: staying connected with the school through the Graduates’ Council and the annual congress.
