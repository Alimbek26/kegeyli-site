Young Academicians club

A club run together with academicians of the Academy of Sciences of Uzbekistan. It was created to develop specialized-school students’ interest in science and their creative thinking.

Why join

Work with real scientists

Meet academicians of the Academy of Sciences and learn from them directly.

Your own project

Take a science project from idea to result, and put what you learn into practice.

Stronger university applications

Research activity and projects are a strong point in applications to universities abroad and in Uzbekistan.

Ready for competitions

Enter “Ixtirochilikka qadam” and other science contests with your project.

Questions and answers

Who can join?

Students of any grade, from 5 to 11.

Does it cost anything?

No, the club is completely free.

When does it meet?

Every other Tuesday at 15:00.

Where?

In the conference room when an academician visits; otherwise in a free classroom or laboratory.

What should I bring?

A notebook. A laptop is recommended if you have one.

How do I join?

Fill in the short application on this website and send it by Telegram or email. Apply to join →
